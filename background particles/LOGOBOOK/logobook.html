стр. 001–024 particle system #1
Фоновая система частиц: Вводная часть / разработка логотипа 
A calm origin field for the opening brand concept section: particles behave like early visual thoughts forming from silence. Create a soft monochrome constellation that slowly gathers into abstract geometric hints of a future logo, then dissolves back into open space. The motion should feel like research, intuition, and discovery: sparse points, subtle orbital drift, gentle breathing scale, and occasional alignment into invisible construction axes. Cursor interaction must be very soft: nearby particles slightly lean away and then return with elastic calm, never chaotic. Keep the composition minimal, premium, slow, and readable, with low contrast and refined grayscale tones.

Prefer low particle velocity, low contrast, no flashing, no aggressive repulsion, and preserve a central quiet reading zone.

стр. 025–105 particle system #2
Фоновая система частиц: Константы фирменного стиля 
A precise modular particle system for brand constants: particles form a quiet geometric construction grid with recurring symbolic modules, proportions, safe zones, and invisible measurement logic. The structure should feel stable, rational, and professional: points align into triangles, circles, axes, and modular rows, then slowly breathe without losing order. Cursor interaction should subtly bend the grid like a soft magnetic field, creating a temporary local distortion that smoothly returns to perfect structure. Use restrained monochrome color with slight tonal variation, no bright effects, no visual noise, and a calm architectural rhythm.

Prefer low particle velocity, low contrast, no flashing, no aggressive repulsion, and preserve a central quiet reading zone.

Фоновая система частиц: Деловая документация 
A paper-and-typography inspired particle background for business documentation: particles should resemble microscopic paper fibers, text baselines, invisible margins, and document layout guides. Create a calm field of horizontal and vertical flows, with particles gently organizing into page-like columns, letter-spacing rhythms, and soft rectangular zones. The animation must be extremely subtle, like air moving across sheets of premium paper. Cursor interaction should create a slow ripple in the document grid, as if the cursor is a soft pressure point under translucent paper. Keep the visual light, elegant, highly readable, and mostly white-gray.

Prefer low particle velocity, low contrast, no flashing, no aggressive repulsion, and preserve a central quiet reading zone.
